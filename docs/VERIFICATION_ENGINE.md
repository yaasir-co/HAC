# Authoritative HAC verification engine

`web/src/lib/verification.ts` separates product identity suggestions from official approval evaluation. `evaluateVerification(input, records)` is a pure evaluator when supplied a fixed timestamp. `verifyProduct(input, {db?})` loads real Prisma records and permits an existing transaction client. Callers must authorize the inspected company first.

Each result retains multiple simultaneous outcomes, all applied rules, actual/expected values, source references, effective timestamp, selected certificate, applicable explicit restrictions, a rules version, and a SHA-256 source-record fingerprint. The fingerprint changes when the loaded catalogue, scope, certificate/history, relationships, licenses or restrictions change. Saved inspection observations preserve the full result snapshot.

A selected product is not approved until a human confirms physical identity. Unknown products are unverified and never inferred to be prohibited. Manufacturer-held certificates may cover retail products; a retailer relationship is required only where the recorded scope explicitly requests it. Exact variants, barcodes, brands and manufacturers are compared. Scope-captured brands/manufacturers prevent later catalogue edits from silently expanding certified scope. Manufacturing facilities are distinct from retail inspection locations.

Effective active certificate renewals take precedence over expired earlier coverage. Certificate status history is evaluated at the inspection timestamp. Incomplete historical status requires review. Physical shelf-life, commercial licenses, HAC certificate dates, and explicit restrictions produce separate outcomes. Date-only physical expiry is the end of that date in Asia/Colombo; DateTime certificate fields are exact instants. Keep certificate and status-history records immutable where they form submitted inspection evidence.

Configuration comes from recorded scope requirements, license required flags, effective dates, explicit restrictions, and authorized data. These engineering rules are not a substitute for formal HAC policy approval. Supplier-specific scope rules, batch-specific restrictions, certificate-scope revision history, and advanced configurable policy authoring need further work. Status and scope mutations must be controlled, audited, and versioned by backend workflows.

The deterministic Vitest suite covers exact approval, physical identity confirmation, brand/manufacturer/barcode/SKU differences, variant scope, all expiry distinctions, valid renewals, historical status, manufacturer-held approval, company relationship validity, manufacturing facilities, explicit/irrelevant/future restrictions, unknown products, simultaneous findings, and explainability. It is not an accuracy evaluation of image recognition.
