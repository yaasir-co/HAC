# HAC branding assets

The user supplied the HAC rectangular wordmark and Sri Lanka circular Halal mark for this application. The matching original web assets were retrieved from HAC's official website:

- `hac-wordmark.png`: https://www.hac.lk/assets/website/img/logo.png
- `hac-halal.png`: https://www.hac.lk/assets/website/img/well-img1-new.png

The 192/512 PNG app icons and 180 PNG Apple touch icon are padded/resized derivatives of the supplied circular mark. They are identification assets for this HAC application; displaying a logo does not approve a product. No new company logos or product photos were invented. Company/product display images are uploaded by authorised HAC administrators.

The `hac-original-v4-192.png`, `hac-original-v4-512.png` and `hac-original-apple-v4.png` derivatives preserve the exact original artwork and aspect ratio with padding. The manifest uses purpose `any`; these are not redesigned or maskable substitutes.
